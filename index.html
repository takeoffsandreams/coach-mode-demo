Welcome to the Coach Mode prototype

This is an early prototype built for testing and feedback. It is not the finished game. The two teams (Harbor City Pilots and Prairie Ridge Stallions) are completely made up.

  • You are the head coach. Before every play, pick a call.
  • The computer suggests the play that gives your team the best chance to win, and tells you why. You can accept it, choose something else, or turn recommendations off with Full manual.
  • The simulation decides what happens, so even a good call can go wrong.
  • Your game saves in this browser automatically. Use Save to file to keep a copy.

Not built yet: timeouts, penalties, onside kicks, individual player ratings, seasons.

Found something confusing, broken or unrealistic? Please tell us in the community.
